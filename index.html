How it plays

Every hole is a race

Forget the scorecard. In Crazy Golf VR your time is the score — take as many shots as you need, just sink it fast.

  1. 01

    Tee off

    Grab your club and swing for real. A beacon of light follows your ball so you never lose it.

  2. 02

    Chase it down

    Walk or teleport after your ball and hit again from wherever it lands. Lose it, and it comes back to its last safe spot.

  3. 03

    Beat the clock

    Sink the putt to stop the timer. Personal bests and world ranks land on the Meta Horizon leaderboards.

Play your way

Solo, squad, or showdown

1 player

Solo

Your run, your record. Clear a course to unlock the next one, then come back to shave off seconds.

2–4 players

Co-op

One shared ball, one shared clock. Anyone can take the next shot — no waiting your turn.

2 v 2

Team race

Each team gets its own ball. Both race for the same cup, and the first team to sink it wins.

Clubs, plungers & dinosaurs

The fairway fights back

  • The golden club. Hatched from an egg in the glade and in your hand for every course after.
  • The plunger gun. Draw it from your hip to ring gongs and drive off anything in your way.
  • Raptors. They hold the fairway. Push them back before they push you around.
  • Pterodactyls. Circling overhead from the forest to the volcano.
